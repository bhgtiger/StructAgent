# Safety, privacy, and licensing

## Outstanding gaps

- Repository code at v2.1.0 is MIT, but model-weight, ESM-2, RNA-FM, Conda-package, and distribution licenses were not established.
- Static source and documentation do not complete an outbound-network audit.
- The CryoAtom2 preprint license does not itself authorize copying/adapting its text into a live skill.

## Never normalize

- Do not recommend wget --no-check-certificate or another TLS-bypass workaround.
- Do not treat Colab as a harmless default. Uploading unpublished, sensitive, controlled-access, or regulated maps/sequences needs a distinct approval and retention/privacy assessment.
- Do not claim a local-looking workflow is fully offline.

## User data

Do not request, store, forward, or upload maps or sequences. Use placeholders in any planning template.

## Escalation

Surface the unresolved gap and request a later explicit approval; do not route around it.
